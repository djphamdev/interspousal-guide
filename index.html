Interspousal Transfer Exclusion Guide

Fixing the 50% improper reassessment after adding a spouse to a deed in LA County

R&T Code §63 Exclusion Applies

The Situation

L & K got married. One spouse owned a home, and they updated the deed in LA County to add the other spouse. The county's TITAN system treated this as a sale and reassessed 50% of the property to fair market value — triggering a massive property tax increase.

The good news: This was a mistake. Under California law, interspousal transfers are statutorily excluded from reassessment.

Why the 50% Reassessment Happened

Normal Rule

Adding someone to a deed = "change of ownership" → reassessment of transferred interest (50%) to current fair market value.

Spousal Exception

Transfers between spouses are explicitly excluded from "change of ownership" under R&T Code §63. The Prop 13 base year value stays intact.

What Went Wrong

  • PCOR (BOE-502-A) was filed (Verification Code K) but likely didn't claim the spousal exclusion
  • TITAN system/analyst didn't recognize the exemption
  • Issued supplemental/escape assessment as if it were a regular sale
  • Ownership Code 2 = "5 - Reappraisable" confirms system treated it as taxable

Can This Be Fixed? Yes — Statute of Limitations & Refunds

Key Timeline

  • Reassessment: October 1, 2021
  • 4-year statute of limitations for escape assessments: expires ~October 2025
  • We're still within the window — act now

Refund Mechanics

  • 3% annual interest on overpaid taxes (statutory rate per R&T Code §5151)
  • Refunds will return taxes to pre-2022 levels (Prop 13 base year value)
  • Escape assessment authority: up to 4 years normally, 8 years if Change in Ownership Statement wasn't filed (R&T Code §532)
  • Since PCOR verification code is "K" (PCOR-Single Parcel), a PCOR was filed — but likely didn't claim the spousal exclusion

Action Plan — What to Do Now

1

Gather Documents

  • Recorded deed adding spouse to title (Document #1501282)
  • Preliminary Change of Ownership Report (PCOR / BOE-502-A) — verification code K confirms one exists
  • Marriage certificate (proof of marital status at time of transfer)
  • All supplemental/escape assessment notices and tax bills showing 50% FMV reassessment
  • Property tax bills from 2022-2024 showing overpayment
2

Contact LA County Assessor's Office

Say this: "This was an interspousal transfer under R&T Code §63; we believe it was mistakenly treated as a sale and reassessed 50% to FMV. Document #1501282, APN 4041-008-021."

Ask for:

  • Review/cancellation of supplemental/escape assessment based on interspousal exclusion
  • What form(s) they want: usually a corrected PCOR and/or written claim for interspousal transfer exclusion
  • Refund claim forms for overpaid taxes + 3% interest

File the Claim & Refund Request

4

If Assessor Won't Cooperate — Appeal

File Application for Changed Assessment with the Assessment Appeals Board (form BOE-305-AH) within 60 days of the mailing date on the escape/supplemental notice or tax bill.

Bring: deed, PCOR, marriage proof, clear timeline of transfer date (10/01/2021) and notice receipt.
